## Activation and validation rules

Every stored update carries its source name/URL/type/authority, retrieval time, source publication time where actually known, effective dates where known, season/financial year where relevant, raw source reference, and verification status. Source links are HTTPS and publisher allowlisted; server fetches enforce time/size limits and validate redirects. No browser scraping, CAPTCHA bypass, or paid API is used.

An HTML structure change or an empty parse does not delete earlier valid records. A stale price retains its original price date and stale badge. An expired scheme cannot appear as active. Source confidence and estate eligibility are separate: an official webpage does not establish that a person satisfies its component rules.

Detailed research evidence and URLs are retained in the accompanying `farm-research-*.md` files when each source review is finalized. The four enabled adapters have actual response and parser tests. Other sources remain disabled until their documented gaps are resolved. Programme/registration evidence is in `farm-research-boards.md`; the linked SPICED September dates are past, and legacy guideline percentages are not activated.
